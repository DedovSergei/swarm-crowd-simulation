# Stair flow in the literature and the calibration targets

The flows a stair carries, as reported in the sources below. **Every value is as reported in the source named and not yet checked against the original**: they were collected from the publications' abstracts, a review's tables and the issuing bodies' own summaries, not all from the full texts.

## Calibration targets

Specific flow per metre of the gangway's clear width, 1.20 m (the incline that the agents' centres span).

| target | ascending | descending | source |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **EMPIRICAL** (the default parameter set) | **1.22 persons/(m s) = 73.2 persons/(m min)** | **1.25 persons/(m s) = 75.0 persons/(m min)** | Burghardt, Seyfried & Klingsch (2013): the measured maximum specific flows on stairs, separately up and down |
| **CONSERVATIVE** (the second parameter set) | **66 persons/(m min) = 1.10 persons/(m s)** | **66 persons/(m min)** | the Green Guide's planning rate for stepped surfaces (6th ed., 2018), the rate the venues' exits were sized with |

The measured spread lies below EMPIRICAL (Fruin's transit stairs 62-66, the SFPE correlation per clear metre of a 1.20 m gangway 45-52): EMPIRICAL is the optimistic end of the measured range.
